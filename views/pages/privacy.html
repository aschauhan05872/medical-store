Med Doorshipp collects personal and health-related information necessary to fulfill pharmacy dispensation and door-delivery services, including name, contact details, shipping address, and medication selection.

Your data is used exclusively to process medication orders, coordinate white-glove delivery, comply with pharmaceutical regulations, and communicate order status. We do not sell personal health information to third parties.

All patient and prescription-related data is handled with clinical-grade confidentiality. Access is restricted to authorized pharmacy and logistics personnel involved in your order.

Order records are retained as required by applicable pharmacy and healthcare regulations. You may request access to or correction of your personal data by contacting privacy@meddoorshipp.com.
