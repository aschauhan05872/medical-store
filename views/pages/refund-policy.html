Refund & Returns Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Pharmaceutical Products

Due to federal and state regulations governing prescription and over-the-counter medications, opened or dispensed pharmaceutical products cannot be returned for resale. Unopened, undamaged items may be eligible for review on a case-by-case basis.

Damaged or Incorrect Shipments

If your medication arrives damaged, incorrect, or compromised during transit, contact us within 48 hours of delivery. Our team will arrange replacement or refund after verification, including photo documentation when applicable.

Cold-Chain & Temperature-Sensitive Items

Temperature-controlled medications that show evidence of cold-chain breach during delivery are eligible for full replacement at no additional cost upon confirmed inspection.

Order Cancellation

Orders may be cancelled before dispensation and shipment processing begins. Once a medication has entered the fulfillment queue, cancellation may not be possible under pharmacy regulations.

Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days to the original payment method. Our concierge team will confirm refund status via email.

How to Request a Refund

Contact Patient Support with your order reference number (e.g. MD-000001) and a description of the issue.
