Shipping & Delivery

White-glove door delivery with temperature-controlled options for sensitive medications.

Standard Delivery

Most orders ship within 1–2 business days after prescription verification. Delivery estimates are provided when your order dispatches. Tracking details are sent via email and SMS.

Cold-Chain Shipping

Temperature-sensitive medications are shipped in validated insulated packaging with chain-of-custody documentation. Signature may be required upon delivery.

Discreet Packaging

All shipments use unmarked exterior packaging. Medication details are never displayed on outer labels.

Delivery Areas

We currently coordinate door delivery throughout the United States. International shipping is available for select wellness supplements — contact our team for eligibility.
